# Enrolment steps at Tala Ridge College (fictional)

Returning students enrol in five steps:
1. Get an advising slip from the department adviser.
2. Encode subjects on the Tala Student Portal.
3. Have the assessment form printed at the Registrar.
4. Pay at least the down payment at the Cashier.
5. Present the official receipt at the Registrar to be marked enrolled.

New students also submit these documents to Admissions: Form 138, a birth certificate photocopy, two 2x2 ID photos, and a good moral certificate.

Change of subjects is done at the Registrar using Form CS-2. It is allowed only during the first two weeks of the term.

Dropping a subject uses Form DS-1 and needs the adviser's signature.

A lost ID is replaced at the Registrar after paying the replacement fee at the Cashier.
