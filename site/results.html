Earlier manual runs are not comparable: two scored 0% because of deployment bugs, one scored 9 of 12 and another 11 of 12 before and after a scoring fix. The scoring is keyword matching. I changed it so that special hyphens and spaces match, which raised the score, so read it as a measure of how well answers match expected wording.
