What I would do next

In priority order:

  1. Add a health check from outside the server. A scheduled test of the public address would catch failures that never reach the gateway, which is the weakness the incident exposed.
  2. Restart the database automatically after a crash, and add a health check to its container. It stayed down after the disk was cleared.
  3. Stop the root cause properly. Keep logs on their own small volume, so a runaway log cannot affect the database.
  4. Encrypt the link from CloudFront to the server. That traffic is plain HTTP today.
  5. Improve quality measurement. Grow the test set beyond 12 questions and stop relying on keyword matching, including cases like the late-enrolment answer that was confidently incomplete.
